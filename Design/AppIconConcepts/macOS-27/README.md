# Sudrf app icon concepts for macOS 27

These are exploratory flattened previews generated for visual direction selection. The active `AppIcon.appiconset` is unchanged.

1. `concept-01-glass-balance.png` — a filled, layered evolution of the current scales.
2. `concept-02-case-layers.png` — saved court files and case tracking.
3. `concept-03-court-timeline.png` — courthouse plus procedural timeline.
4. `concept-04-docket-compass.png` — case discovery and progress tracking.
5. `concept-05-terracotta-balance.png` — the scales direction in the court-site palette.
6. `concept-06-court-dockets.png` — layered case files in parchment, taupe, terracotta, and court red.
7. `concept-07-terracotta-orbit.png` — the docket orbit direction in the court-site palette.
8. `concept-08-parchment-balance-bold.png` — deep court-red scales on parchment.
9. `concept-09-parchment-balance-light.png` — a lighter geometric balance on parchment.
10. `concept-10-parchment-dockets-status.png` — front-facing dockets with a status accent.
11. `concept-11-parchment-dockets-clean.png` — quiet dockets without a separate status dot.
12. `concept-12-balance-strengthened.png` — concept 09 with stronger geometry and clean negative space.
13. `concept-13-balance-filled-bowls.png` — concept 09 with compact filled pans.
14. `concept-14-dockets-clearer.png` — concept 11 with stronger folder hierarchy and a larger court mark.
15. `concept-15-dockets-inlaid.png` — concept 11 with a lighter folder and an inlaid terracotta court.

The selected direction should be redrawn as separate vector layers and assembled in Apple Icon Composer. Do not treat the baked visual effects in these previews as final layer artwork.

The terracotta palette was sampled from a current general-jurisdiction court website: `#970F00`, `#A0282B`, `#CC6600`, `#FDF5E9`, `#E4D9CD`, `#B0997B`, and `#755A57`.
